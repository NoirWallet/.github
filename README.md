# Noir Wallet

**Privacy for payments. Privacy for DeFi.**

Noir Wallet is a privacy-first, self-custodial wallet designed to bring privacy to everyday onchain finance.

Starting with Zcash and expanding across multiple blockchains, Noir Wallet enables users to manage shielded assets, receive and transfer funds privately, and access DeFi with greater control over their financial privacy.

## What We're Building

### 1. Private Payments

Receive and transfer supported assets with privacy-focused workflows.

- Shielded Zcash transactions
- Privacy-focused receiving and transfers
- Multichain privacy infrastructure

### 2. Private DeFi

Access DeFi while reducing the direct link between your primary wallet identity and onchain financial activity.

- Public and Private wallet modes
- Privacy-preserving asset migration
- Access to existing DeFi protocols through separate execution accounts

Privacy protections depend on the underlying network and protocol. Private Mode does not make all onchain activity invisible.

### 3. Self-Custody

Your assets. Your keys. Your control.

Noir Wallet is built around self-custody, with private keys managed locally by the user.
